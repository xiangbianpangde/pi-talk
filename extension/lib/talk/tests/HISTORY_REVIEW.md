# Local history semantic review — provisional, not acceptance

## Protocol and privacy

The user authorized read-only access to local Pi history. Twenty distinct task traces were inspected with user intent, original final reply and bounded tool observations. Project names, house identifiers, session ids, personal paths, URLs and credentials are excluded from this document. Source history was not edited or committed.

This is a **single-assistant qualitative review** and one-pass proposed rewriting exercise. It is not independent fact verification, a blinded A/B experiment, an execution of the Information Engine's semantic producer, or a live provider benchmark. Some original replies and tool observations were bounded, so missing information cannot automatically be scored as omission. A tool's success flag and another assistant's statement are not independent acceptance evidence.

The five previously presented A/B examples were both assistant-authored. The user's five B choices establish a status/conclusion-first presentation preference only. They do not establish engine accuracy.

## Findings, not quality scores

1. Status and scope need to precede completion language (C01, C13, C14, C19).
2. Technical/transport success is not visual, business or delivery acceptance (C07, C08, C12, C17, C20).
3. Contradictions and retractions are high-value information and must survive summarization (C07–C10).
4. Hypotheses need attribution and a concrete next verification, not a confident root-cause label (C05, C16).
5. Image capability failures are explicit blockers even when filesystem reads succeed (C09, C15).
6. Proposed rewrites are shorter but may remove useful detail. No numeric reading-value or accuracy improvement is claimed without full-source scoring and independent review.
